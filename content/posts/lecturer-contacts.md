## Contact Etiquette

- Use official email for academic matters.
- Include your name, NIM, class, and course.
- Keep the tone polite and formal.
- Respect working hours — avoid contacting outside reasonable times.
- Allow 1–2 days for a response.
- For sensitive matters, use official department channels.

Phone numbers are an alternative contact, not the primary channel.

## FAQ

**Why are some lecturers missing from the list?**
They may be from other departments or not yet registered. Contact the Informatics Department secretariat.

**Can I use phone numbers for consultation?**
For academic matters, prioritize official email and e-learning.

**What is NIK?**
Employee ID Number (Nomor Induk Karyawan), used for official correspondence.

**The data is inaccurate, what should I do?**
Verify through the official department website or e-learning, and confirm with the secretariat.
